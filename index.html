A little about me

Curiosity into capability.

I'm Naveen Kumar, an engineer and researcher pursuing a PhD at IIT Indore. My academic background includes a B.Tech from MNNIT Allahabad and an M.Tech from NIT Jalandhar, with a focus on signal processing and machine learning.
