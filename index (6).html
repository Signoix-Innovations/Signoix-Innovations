A little about me

Curiosity into capability.

I'm Naveen Kumar, an engineer and researcher pursuing a PhD at IIT Indore. My academic background includes a B.Tech from MNNIT Allahabad and an M.Tech from NIT Jalandhar, with a focus on signal processing and machine learning.

My professional experience spans business analytics at Tredence Inc. and machine-learning R&D at Hindware Home Innovation Limited. Across these roles and my independent projects, I've explored AI systems for monitoring, healthcare, computer vision, and intelligent control.

Beyond technical work, I enjoy teaching, mentoring students, travelling, trekking, and playing sports. I'm interested in building technologies that connect strong engineering with practical impact.
